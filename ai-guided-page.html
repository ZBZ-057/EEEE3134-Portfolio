Closed-Loop Control Design for a 50 W High-Frequency SMPS

Objective Model a high-frequency switched-mode power supply, design its loop compensation and PCB, and test the prototype.

  • PLECS
  • KiCad
  • LCR meter
  • Oscilloscope

Bench measurement

The inductance deviation was within 10%.

Thermal modeling

The model indicated that MOSFET junction temperatures remained within safe limits.

Design targets

Convert 30 V AC to regulated 10 V DC, with output ripple below 0.2 V.

Technical details
Modeling and compensation
Built a two-switch forward-converter model in PLECS and designed Type I and Type III compensators using frequency-response analysis. Investigated current-mode control in simulation to improve transient response.
PCB design
Designed the power PCB layout and two-layer board stack-up in KiCad. Used high-frequency routing and single-point grounding to address EMI.
Testing and analysis
Characterized component parameters with an LCR meter, measured ripple with an oscilloscope, and assessed MOSFET junction temperatures through thermal modeling.

Radar Speed Measurement System and Signal Conditioning Platform

Objective Condition low-amplitude X-band radar signals for speed measurement and display the resulting data on a PC.

  • LTspice
  • STM32 Nucleo
  • Embedded C
  • USART

Reported outcomes

  • 0.4%Cutoff-frequency deviation
  • 50 dBNoise attenuation at 10 Hz
  • 0.12 m/sHuman motion detected down to this speed

Speed measurements closely matched the theoretical Doppler values.

Technical details
Analog front end
Selected low-noise op-amps, precision resistors, and precision capacitors. Simulated the circuit in LTspice and built a two-stage op-amp conditioning circuit with a fifth-order Butterworth filter.
Waveform shaping and sampling
Used a Schmitt trigger for waveform shaping and designed a level-shifting circuit for the STM32 Nucleo sampling range.
Visualization
Implemented real-time PC visualization of speed data using embedded C and USART communication.
